// 01 — THE COMPANY

ABOUT KAIRO RECORDS

WE DON’T JUST PRODUCE MUSIC.
WE BUILD WORLDS AROUND IT.

Founded at the nexus of cinematic storytelling and acoustic vanguardism, KAIRO is an independent music production company, record label, and audio-visual house based in Cairo.

We fuse the irreplaceable weight of analog circuitry with state-of-the-art spatial synthesis. From subterranean electronic records and world-class live tracking to original motion picture scores, every frequency we sculpt carries intention, narrative depth, and cultural resonance.
